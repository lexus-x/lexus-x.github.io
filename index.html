M.S. thesis

A wavelet action head for robot models

SmolVLA generates robot actions with a 99.88M-parameter flow-matching expert. I designed a head that replaces it with a Wavelet Neural Operator and produces each action chunk in a single pass, built on prior project work.

Flow-matching expert
99.88M
Wavelet head
8.43M

Success is less clear. A pre-registered paired test of one seed on 8,000 new LIBERO placements gave 83.2% against the expert's 80.8%, but another seed had the expert ahead. A regression head of the same size could not be distinguished from mine, and 2 of 5 benchmarks passed non-inferiority.

M.S. research

Why robot policies fail, and what doesn't fix it

I built a reproducible baseline for the public SmolVLA checkpoint on LIBERO-Long: 42.3% ± 2.1% success over 3 seeds and 600 episodes. A one-seed pilot review of 53 failed rollouts, labeled by vision models, found 30% missed grasps and 28% that stalled after the first subtask.

Probes on the frozen policy's latent layers detected the first object placement with 0.876 held-out ROC-AUC (shuffled control 0.49), so the signal is there. Acting on it was harder. Both modules I tested missed their pre-registered gates: an oracle-triggered retry pilot gained 3.3 points against a 5-point bar, and stage feedback lost 11.7 points.

2026

FishONet: 3rd place, CV4Ecology 2026 Challenge

I led the four-person team CWNU AIX to 3rd on the leaderboard among 103 participants. The benchmark covers 17,393 fish species, 11,598 of them with no photos. We scored 53.761% overall: 77.00% on photographed species and 23.77% on unseen ones.

The system ensembles three BioCLIP-2.5 backbones and joins closed-set classification with open-set retrieval through a 12-feature logistic router. A validation leak showed a +13.374-point proxy gain for a change worth +0.006 on the real score. We removed the leak and obtained a confirmed +0.213 leaderboard gain.
